# Forest Ecosystem Simulation

## Project Topic
**Forest Ecosystem Simulation**

A simulation of a forest environment where different species of plants and animals interact with each other and with changing environmental conditions.

## Project Description

The goal of this project is to create a simulation of a forest ecosystem on a predefined 20x20 board. Various species of plants and animals can be added to the environment, and their positions and environmental conditions can be adjusted during the simulation.

Key features of the simulation:
- Plants and animals are initialized with 100% health, energy, or other relevant parameters.
- Initial water and sunlight levels are set to 100%.
- Environmental conditions (e.g. weather) affect plants, animals, and the ecosystem:
  - Example: Rain increases the water level, which is essential for plant hydration.
- Plants and animals interact, affecting each other's health and energy.
- If a plant or animal reaches 0% in a vital parameter (e.g. energy, health), it is removed from the board.
- The simulation collects data about species population and environmental status at each stage.
- After the simulation ends, results are saved as a CSV file for further analysis.

---

## Features Overview

- Interactions between different plant and animal species  
- Changing weather conditions (e.g., sunny, rainy, cloudy)  
- Real-time population tracking and environmental metrics
